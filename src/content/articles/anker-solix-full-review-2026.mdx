Anker spent a decade building its reputation on phone chargers, power banks, and cables — the accessories you grab at the airport when you forgot your charger. So when the company launched the **SOLIX** line of full-size portable power stations, the question was fair: can a consumer electronics brand compete with the established players in serious off-grid power?

Three models into the lineup — the C1000, F2000, and F3800 — the answer is a clear yes. The SOLIX series brings Anker's signature strengths (fast charging, polished apps, solid build quality) to a category that's been dominated by EcoFlow, Jackery, and Bluetti. But each model targets a different user, and choosing the wrong one means either overpaying for capacity you don't need or coming up short when the power goes out.

Here's a complete breakdown of the Anker SOLIX lineup in 2026 — who each model is for, how they compare, and which one fits your use case.

## Anker SOLIX C1000 — Best for Beginners and Weekend Warriors

The **C1000** is the entry point of the SOLIX lineup, but "entry point" sells it short. With 1,056Wh of capacity and 1,800W of continuous output, it's a genuinely capable unit that covers the most common use cases: weekend camping, day-trip power, CPAP overnight, and keeping essentials running during short outages.

### Key Specs:
- **1,056Wh LiFePO4 battery** — enough for a mini fridge (8+ hours), CPAP (2–3 nights without humidifier), or charging laptops and phones for a family over a weekend.
- **1,800W continuous / 2,400W surge** — handles 90% of household devices including microwaves, coffee makers, and most power tools.
- **HyperFlash charging:** 0–80% in 45 minutes from a wall outlet. Full charge in under 60 minutes. This is where Anker's consumer electronics DNA shows — no one in the industry charges faster.
- **13 ports:** 5 AC outlets, 2 USB-C (100W), 2 USB-A, 2 DC, 1 car outlet. More than enough for a group campsite or family emergency setup.
- **28.7 lbs** — heavy enough to feel substantial, light enough for one person to carry from car to campsite.

### Real-World Use:
For a weekend camping trip, the C1000 powers a 12V mini fridge during the day and LED lights + device charging at night, with enough headroom to run a coffee maker in the morning. For a short home outage (2–6 hours), it keeps your refrigerator, router, a few lights, and your laptop running long enough for the grid to come back.

### Who It's For:
Campers, weekenders, CPAP users, and homeowners who want basic outage protection without spending four figures.

## Anker SOLIX F2000 — Best for Home Backup and Extended Off-Grid

The **F2000** is where the SOLIX line gets serious. At 2,048Wh — roughly double the C1000 — and 2,400W of continuous output, it moves from "weekend camping" into "multi-day outage protection" and "full RV support" territory.

### Key Specs:
- **2,048Wh LiFePO4 battery** — double the C1000. Powers a full-size refrigerator for 12–16 hours, runs power tools all day, or supports a full RV setup overnight.
- **2,400W continuous / 3,600W surge** — handles well pumps, larger power tools, and induction cooktops. The surge rating covers most motor startup spikes.
- **Expandable:** The F2000 supports Anker's expansion battery (2,048Wh add-on), doubling total capacity to 4,096Wh. You can buy the base unit now and add capacity later as needs grow.
- **HyperFlash charging:** 0–80% in 1 hour. Double the capacity of the C1000 with only 15 minutes more charging time.
- **UPS mode:** 20ms switchover — fast enough to keep computers and sensitive electronics running during a brownout. The F2000 can serve as an uninterruptible power supply for your home office.
- **63.9 lbs** — you'll want the wheeled cart accessory if you're moving it frequently. This is a two-person lift for most people.

### Real-World Use:
During a 12-hour power outage, the F2000 runs a full-size refrigerator (intermittent cycling), Wi-Fi router, a few lamps, and device charging — with capacity to spare. On a job site, it powers a miter saw and circular saw through a full workday. In an RV, it replaces a generator for all-night power without the noise.

### Who It's For:
Homeowners in areas with frequent outages, RV owners, job site contractors, and anyone who needs multi-day off-grid power.

## Anker SOLIX F3800 — Best for Whole-Home Backup and 240V Appliances

The **F3800** is Anker's flagship — and it competes directly with the EcoFlow DELTA Pro Ultra and Bluetti EP500 for the title of best home backup power station in 2026. With 3,840Wh of base capacity (expandable to 7,680Wh) and a massive 6,000W inverter, it crosses the threshold from "portable power station" to "home energy system."

### Key Specs:
- **3,840Wh LiFePO4 battery (expandable to 7,680Wh)** — runs essential home circuits for 1–2 days without recharging. With solar input and conservative usage, it can sustain critical loads indefinitely.
- **6,000W continuous / 12,000W surge** — this is the spec that changes the game. The F3800 can run **240V appliances** including well pumps, electric dryers, EV chargers (Level 1), and whole-home air conditioning. Most portable power stations top out at 120V — the F3800's 240V support puts it in a different category.
- **120V/240V split-phase output:** Connect two F3800 units for 12,000W of 240V output — enough to back up an entire home's essential circuits through a transfer switch.
- **HyperFlash charging:** 0–80% in 1.2 hours from a 240V outlet. That's over 3,000Wh in roughly the time it takes to watch a movie.
- **Home integration:** Compatible with Anker's Home Power Panel for automatic whole-home backup. When the grid goes down, the F3800 takes over within 20ms — fast enough that your lights don't even flicker.
- **Solar input:** Up to 2,400W of solar input. A full recharge from solar in 2–3 hours of good sun. Realistically, a 1,200W panel array recharges the base unit in a single day even in less-than-perfect conditions.
- **130 lbs** — this is a stationary unit. It has wheels and a handle, but "portable" is relative. You're not hauling this to a campsite.

### Real-World Use:
Connected to a home transfer switch, the F3800 powers refrigerators, freezers, lights, internet, well pump, and HVAC for an entire day during an outage. With solar panels feeding it during the day, it can sustain critical circuits through multi-day blackouts. For off-grid cabins and workshops, it replaces a gas generator entirely — silently, with zero fuel costs.

### Who It's For:
Homeowners who want serious outage protection, off-grid cabin owners, and anyone running 240V appliances who doesn't want to install a permanently mounted battery system.

## Which SOLIX Should You Buy?

| Your Situation | Best SOLIX Model |
|---|---|
| Camping weekends, CPAP, light backup under 12 hours | **C1000** |
| Multi-day camping, RV power, job sites, 12–24 hour home backup | **F2000** |

If budget allows, the **F2000** is the sweet spot: enough capacity for serious use cases without the cost and weight of the flagship. The expansion option means you can start with 2,048Wh and grow to 4,096Wh later. The C1000 is the right pick if you know your needs are modest — and you'll appreciate the lighter weight and faster deployment. The F3800 is for when the power going out isn't an inconvenience but a genuine problem.

## The Bottom Line

Anker's SOLIX lineup has earned its place alongside EcoFlow, Jackery, and Bluetti in the 2026 power station market. The C1000 is the best fast-charging mid-size unit for beginners and weekenders. The F2000 delivers home-backup-grade power with expansion flexibility at a competitive price. And the F3800's 240V output — at a price below the EcoFlow DELTA Pro Ultra — makes it the most accessible whole-home backup option on the market.

The charging speed advantage is real: when the grid comes back for 45 minutes, the C1000 is ready to go again while competitors are still refilling. That alone has saved more than one camping trip and made more than one outage manageable.
