## Fixed vs Portable: The Core Tradeoff

| Factor | Fixed Rooftop Panels | Portable Ground Panels |
|---|---|---|
| **Stealth** | Low profile, invisible from ground | Visible setup, must deploy and stow |
| **Sun tracking** | Fixed angle — limited by roof orientation | Can reposition to track sun throughout the day |
| **Shade vulnerability** | Parked under trees = zero production | Move panels into sun regardless of van position |
| **Installation** | Permanent — drilling, wiring, sealing | No installation — unfold and plug in |
| **Security** | Always attached, difficult to steal | Must secure or stow when away from van |
| **Aerodynamics** | Adds wind resistance and slight MPG penalty | No aerodynamic impact |
| **Cost** | Higher (hardware + installation) | Lower (standalone panels + cables) |

Most experienced van lifers run **both**: fixed panels as the primary always-on system, and a portable panel for supplementary charging when the van is parked in shade or when the roof needs extra capacity.

## Sizing Your System: A Realistic Power Budget

Before buying anything, calculate what you actually need. Here is a realistic daily power budget for a typical van life setup:

| Appliance | Watts | Hours/Day | Daily Wh |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12V compressor fridge | 40W | 8 (cycling) | 320Wh |
| Laptop + monitor | 80W | 4 | 320Wh |
| LED lights (4 bulbs) | 20W | 6 | 120Wh |
| Phone + device charging | 30W | 4 | 120Wh |
| Maxxfan roof fan | 30W | 8 | 240Wh |
| Water pump | 50W | 0.5 | 25Wh |
| **Daily Total** | — | — | **~1,145Wh** |

For this budget, using 5 peak sun hours and factoring in 80% system efficiency (wiring losses, inverter efficiency):

- **Minimum solar array:** 1,145Wh / 5 hours / 0.8 = ~290W of panels
- **Recommended solar array:** 400W (provides buffer for cloudy days)
- **Minimum battery bank:** 1,145Wh x 2 days of autonomy = ~2,300Wh (200Ah at 12V LiFePO4)

A 400W rooftop setup with a 2,400Wh battery bank will cover this budget comfortably with room to spare for occasional high-draw items like an induction cooktop or Starlink.

## Van Life Solar Design Principles

**1. MPPT over PWM, always.** Maximum Power Point Tracking controllers extract up to 30% more energy than Pulse Width Modulation in the variable conditions of mobile solar — partial shade, changing angles, varying temperatures. The price difference has narrowed enough that MPPT is the only sensible choice for van builds.

**2. Size your battery bank for 2–3 days of autonomy.** Solar production drops to near zero on heavy overcast days. Your battery needs to bridge those gaps. A 2-day buffer at minimum; 3 days if you work remotely and cannot afford a dead battery.

**3. Monitor your state of charge obsessively.** Install a shunt-based battery monitor (Victron BMV-712 or similar). Voltage-based readings are unreliable for LiFePO4 batteries, which maintain nearly flat voltage until nearly depleted.

**4. Angle matters more than you think.** Flat-mounted rooftop panels lose 15–25% of potential output compared to panels angled toward the sun. If your roof allows it, install tilt brackets. The efficiency gain pays for itself over the life of the system.
