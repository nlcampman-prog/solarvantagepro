## Game Day Load Calculation: How Much Power Do You Need?

To select the perfect generator, you must calculate the total wattage of the appliances you plan to run simultaneously. Here is a typical tailgating power setup:

*   **50-inch LED TV:** 100 Watts
*   **Bluetooth Party Speaker:** 50 Watts
*   **Pellet Grill (Traeger):** 300W during startup (approx. 5 minutes), 50W continuously
*   **Slow Cooker (Crock-Pot):** 200 Watts
*   **Electric Griddle / Hot Plate:** 1200–1500 Watts
*   **Smartphone Chargers:** 15 Watts each

**The Golden Rule:** For a typical 4-to-6-hour tailgating party running a TV, speaker, slow cooker, and pellet grill, you need a power station with a battery capacity of **750Wh to 1,000Wh** and an inverter output of at least **1,000 Watts**. If you plan to run heavy-duty heating appliances like electric griddles or blenders, look for an inverter that can output **1,500W to 2,000W**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Can a solar generator run a tailgating slow cooker?
Yes, absolutely! A typical slow cooker runs on about **200 Watts** on high setting. A 1,000Wh solar generator like the **[EcoFlow DELTA 2](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B97DDFY9?tag=momsavesdeals-20)** can run a slow cooker continuously for nearly **5 hours**, or up to **8 hours** on low setting, without needing any solar recharging.

### Do I need solar panels plugged in during the game?
You don't *need* them, but they are highly recommended! Plugs are limited at stadium lots, and having a 100W or 200W solar panel propped up against your truck bed allows you to harvest clean solar energy in real-time, offsetting the draw of your TV and speakers and keeping your battery at 100% all afternoon.
